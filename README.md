# -McDonald-s-Sales-Dashboard_Excel

Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates transactional sales performance across McDonald's menu categories from January 1, 2023 to March 31, 2023 (12,097 valid order items across 5,370 unique orders). The objective is to identify top revenue drivers, operational rush hours, and consumer purchasing patterns to optimize inventory, kitchen staffing, and high-margin bundling.

Questions:
1. What is the total sales revenue for each category of menu items?
2. How many orders are placed each day?
3. Which menu item is the most frequently ordered?
4. What is the total revenue generated by menu items?
5. How does the revenue of each category compare over months?
6. What is the average number of items per order?
7. How do order volumes vary by time of day?
8. How do sales trends differ across weekdays and weekends?
9. How does the sales performance vary by category over different months?
10. Compare the sales of top 5 menu items.

Key Solutions to Project Questions
Question / Metric	Analytical Findings & Solution Details
1. Total Sales Revenue by Category	• Burger: ~$20,900+ (Primary sales & revenue engine)
• Chicken: ~$12,400+
• Pasta: ~$9,100+
• Sandwich: ~$8,200+
• Sides & Fries: ~$4,800 combined
• Breakfast, Shakes, Wraps, Salad: Balance of revenue
Key Takeaway: Burgers & Chicken generate over 50% of aggregate sales.

2. Daily Order Volume	• Average of ~58 to 60 orders placed per day across the 90-day Q1 period.
• Order counts oscillate between 40 orders on slower mid-week days and 80+ orders on peak weekend days.

3. Most Frequently Ordered Menu Item	• Big Mac and Bulgogi Burger lead overall unit transaction frequency among main courses.
• French Fries (Large) leads attachment rate across all sides and snacks.

4. Total Revenue Generated	• Total gross sales across the 3-month dataset reached ~$61,000 – $62,000.

5 & 9. Category Revenue & Trends Across Months	• Sales maintained consistent momentum across Q1, averaging ~$20,000 to ~$21,000 per month.
• March recorded the highest transaction count and revenue, led by sustained gains in Burgers, Chicken, and Pasta.

6. Average Basket Size (Items per Order)	• 2.25 items per order (12,097 total items ordered across 5,370 unique transactions).

7. Order Volume by Time of Day	• Lunch Rush (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM): Peak window accounting for ~34% of daily transactions.
• Dinner Rush (5:00 PM – 7:30 PM): Secondary surge capturing ~30% of daily transactions.
• Afternoon (2:00 PM – 5:00 PM) & Late Night: Lower volume windows suited for off-peak promotions.

8. Weekday vs. Weekend Trends	• Weekends (Fri – Sun): Feature significantly higher basket sizes and Average Order Value (AOV) driven by multi-item family and group orders.
• Weekdays (Mon – Thu): Characterized by higher velocity, single-item lunch-rush combos.

9. • Core Revenue Anchors: Burgers ($21,459.99) and Chicken ($12,283.45) collectively account for over 55%
of gross sales every single month, exhibiting unmatched customer loyalty and demand stability.
• March Rebound &amp; Peak Performance: March generated the highest quarterly sales ($20,913.14) and unit
volume (4,142 items), driven by month-over-month growth in Sandwiches (+14.1%) and Chicken (+8.1%).
• February Calendar Adjustment: February recorded a modest dip in nominal sales ($19,484.12) due solely
to having 28 calendar days. On a daily run-rate basis, sales were actually slightly higher at ~$695.86/day
compared to ~$664.73/day in January.
• Wraps Outlier / Counter-Trend: While most categories dipped slightly in February due to the shorter
calendar month, Wraps surged to 129 units ($470.71) compared to 100 units ($365.00) in January (+29%
growth).
• High Ticket-to-Volume Efficiency (Pasta): Pasta represented 15.0% of total revenue ($9,147.14) while
accounting for only 9.0% of total items sold, reflecting its high average unit price point ($7.99–$8.99).
